# Dashamsha (D10)

Each 30° Rashi is divided into ten parts of exactly 3°. Parts are numbered 1–10 using half-open intervals.

The Parashari starting sign is:

- odd-numbered Rashi: the source Rashi itself;
- even-numbered Rashi: the ninth Rashi from the source.

Counting proceeds forward from that start. The degree within D10 is the fraction within the source 3° segment multiplied by ten.

Examples: 7° Aries is the third Dashamsha and maps to Gemini; 1° Taurus maps to Capricorn; 25° Virgo is the ninth Dashamsha and maps to Capricorn.

D10 Lagna and houses require a reliable birth time. The application does not substitute noon or any other time when it is unknown.
